Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This section provides answers to questions that you may have on AI

Is AI completely safe to use?

While AI can be a powerful tool, it is important to use it responsibly and ethically. There are potential risks associated with AI, such as bias in algorithms and privacy concerns. It is important to follow guidelines and best practices when implementing AI in education to ensure that it is used safely and effectively.

Is AI completely accurate?

AI is not 100 percent accurate all of the time. If it doesnt know specific information it will make up a plausible sounding answer that isnt true this is called hallucination. It is important to fact check any information provided by AI and to use it as a tool to support learning rather than relying on it as the sole source of information.

What are the limitations of AI?

AI has several limitations, including the potential for bias in algorithms, privacy concerns, and the inability to fully understand context or nuance in human language. It is important to be aware of these limitations when using AI.

Is AI going to take over the world?

Probably not.
